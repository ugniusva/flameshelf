PDF Merger

Join PDFs into one document in the order you choose.

Drop PDFs here

PDF documents · up to 120 MB total

    Files are merged from top to bottom. Use the arrows to change their order.

    Example

    Add your cover sheet and report, move the cover sheet to the top, and select Merge PDFs.

    Advertisement
    Advertisement

    Combine a cover, report, and attachments in order

    Add the PDFs and move the cover to the top, followed by the report and attachments. The order in the file list becomes the order of the merged document.

    Each input contributes its pages in their original order. To insert an attachment halfway through a report, first split the report into two files, then put the attachment between them.
